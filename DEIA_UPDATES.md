## EOHHS Office of DEIA Updates

**Effective Date:** 2026-05-05

MassAbility recently received the following information from EOHHS Office of Diversity, Equity, Inclusion & Access (ODEIA) regarding organizational changes with an emphasis to standardize ADA triage and compliance structure and to ensure federal compliance and timely-decision making regarding disability-related accommodation requests and equity initiatives.

MassAbility's Designated Agency Support Team primary leads are:

### ADA Coordinator

- Email: stacy.hart@mass.gov
